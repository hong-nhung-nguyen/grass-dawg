Design a mobile app UI (iOS/Android, portrait) for "Grass Dawg", a virtual
pet dog game that gets people outside to explore their city. The style is
playful, bright and friendly, with rounded shapes, soft shadows, and a
cute 3D cartoon art style for the dog (chunky proportions, big expressive
eyes, soft fur shading). Use a fresh outdoor colour palette: grass green,
sky blue and warm sunshine yellow, with clean white cards.

Design these screens:                                               

1. Onboarding: Choose your dog
A carousel of 6 cartoon dog breeds to pick from: Shiba Inu, Golden
Retriever, Corgi, Pug, Border Collie and Dachshund. Each card shows the
dog in a happy idle pose, the breed name, and a short personality tag
(e.g. "Curious explorer", "Loves water"). A name input field and a large
"Adopt" button at the bottom.

2. Home screen (indoors, sulking)
The dog sits facing away from the viewer toward a wall in a cosy room,
looking bored. A speech bubble says "Take me outside!". Below, a large
camera button labelled "Check if we're outside". Small stat bars at the
top: Happiness, Energy and Level.

3. AR camera view (outdoors)
Full-screen live camera feed of a real city street. The cartoon dog
stands on the ground in the scene as an AR overlay, casting a soft
shadow so it looks grounded in the real world. A small reaction bubble
above the dog (e.g. drinking from a water bowl, "Slurp!"). Minimal
floating controls only: a round shutter-style "Look" button at the
bottom centre, a back button top left, and stat bars top right. Keep
the camera feed dominant and the UI very light.

4. Side quest screen (dog hidden)
Mostly empty, calm screen with a soft gradient background. Only the
dog's tail peeking out from behind a bush illustration. A short quest
card: "Find a fountain and show me!" with a speaker icon to replay the
quest aloud. One small "I found it" camera button. The screen should
feel quiet and encourage putting the phone away.

5. Quest complete
The dog jumps out happily with a reward item (e.g. a tiny umbrella)
floating above it, confetti, and a "+Happiness" badge. A button
"Keep exploring".

6. Diary
A scrapbook-style journal. Entries newest first, each with a date,
a small photo thumbnail from the outing, a short handwritten-style
diary entry written in the dog's voice, and mini stat changes. A
collection shelf at the top showing earned reward items.

Use a consistent bottom navigation with three tabs: Home, Explore
(camera) and Diary. Show the same chosen dog across all screens for
consistency, in different poses: sulking, idle, excited, drinking,
hiding and celebrating. The chosen dog is a Golden Retriever named
Pickle, so screen 1 opens on the Golden Retriever card with the
personality tag "Loves water", which ties into the fountain quest
in screen 4.
